Shoulder Check

Turn your head to steer. Dodge the blocks, chase the stars. Your eyes do the work from the edge of your vision.

  1. Prop the phone or tablet upright at eye level and stand an arm's length away.
  2. Turn your head to move. Hold the turn to hold the lane. Shoulders stay square to the screen.
  3. Read the lanes from the corner of your eye. Avoid red blocks, collect gold stars.
  4. Choose Opposite mapping to make a left turn move you right.

Performance
  • Edge Alert. Head stays centred. Snap toward real targets at the screen edges, ignore decoys.
  • Rule Switch. Turn toward gold, away from red. Then the rule flips.
  • Snapshot. Check your shoulder, look back, point to what moved.
Rehab and therapy, clinician-directed
  • Range Builder. Follow a slow target to the edge of comfortable range and hold.
  • Slow Lanes. Shoulder Check at a seated pace with large objects and small turns.
  • Steady Gaze. Eyes on a centre target while the head turns to a set beat.
Hands-free access
  • Bubble Pop. Point with your nose and dwell to pop.
  • Trace. Steer a dot along a path with small, steady head movement.
  • One Switch. Timing games where any single movement or sound is the button.
